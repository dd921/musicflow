Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

MusicFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

When you use MusicFlow, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Username and securely hashed password when you create an account
  • Strava Data: Activity data including workout metrics (distance, duration, heart rate, pace, cadence, power) when you connect your Strava account
  • Spotify Data: Recently played tracks and listening history when you connect your Spotify account
  • OAuth Tokens: Access and refresh tokens to maintain your connections to Strava and Spotify

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Display your Strava activities with overlaid Spotify track information
  • Store your Spotify listening history to match with past activities
  • Maintain your authenticated sessions with Strava and Spotify

We do not:

  • Share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Train AI models with your data
  • Display your data to other users

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely and we implement appropriate technical measures to protect it. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted, and passwords are securely hashed.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you maintain an active account. Spotify track history is stored locally to enable matching with older activities.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Disconnect: Revoke access to Strava or Spotify at any time through the app or directly through those services
  • Withdraw Consent: You can disconnect your accounts or delete your MusicFlow account at any time

Third-Party Services

MusicFlow integrates with:

  • Strava: We access your activity data through the Strava API. See Strava's Privacy Policy
  • Spotify: We access your listening history through the Spotify API. See Spotify's Privacy Policy

Data Deletion

When you disconnect Strava or Spotify, we delete the associated OAuth tokens. When you delete your account, we delete all your data including:

  • Your account credentials
  • All OAuth tokens
  • All stored Spotify track history

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at:
