📊 About Song History

  • Tracks are automatically stored when you view activities
  • Storage persists across sessions, overcoming Spotify's ~50 track API limit
  • Duplicate tracks (same song + timestamp) are automatically skipped
  • Same song played at different times is stored as separate entries
  • Stored tracks are used to match with activities from any time period
